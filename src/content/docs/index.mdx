## What this is

Most explanations of a data structure give you a definition, a library demo, three
practice problems, and the sentence "insertion is O(log n)". You finish knowing what
the thing is called and nothing about when it will hurt you.

Every page here follows the same spine, and two of the sections are the ones usually
missing:

1. **Intuition** — the mental model, before any code.
2. **Visual** — an interactive widget at the exact sentence where the idea gets hard.
3. **Mechanics** — the operations, in Python and TypeScript.
4. **Complexity** — _derived_. If a page claims `O(n)`, it shows the sum.
5. **When NOT to use it** — the conditions under which this is the wrong choice.
6. **Real-world usage** — where it actually appears in systems you use.
7. **Failure modes** — how it breaks in production, and what the symptom looks like.
8. **Practice problems** — with solutions in both languages.
9. **Interview answers** — the short version, said well.
